Privacy Policy — Balloons Popper

Last updated: August 31, 2026
App: Balloons Popper (com.devendra.balloonspopper)
Publisher: Devendra

This privacy policy describes how Balloons Popper handles information.

Summary

Balloons Popper is a children’s game. It does not collect personal information, show ads, use analytics, or require an account.

Information we collect

We do not collect:

  • names, emails, or contact details
  • location
  • photos, microphone, or camera data
  • advertising IDs
  • analytics or crash-reporting data from your device to our servers

The app has no internet permission and does not send data to remote servers.

Information stored on the device

The app may store only these settings locally on the device:

  • star count / unlocked game modes
  • whether sound is on or off

This information stays on the device and is removed if the app is uninstalled.

Children

Balloons Popper is designed for children. Because we do not collect personal information and do not use ads or tracking, the app is intended to be suitable for family play under Google Play Families policies when configured accordingly in Play Console.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate advertising, analytics, social login, or payment SDKs.

Device text-to-speech may be used for spoken prompts. That feature is provided by the device/OS and is not used by us to collect data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

Contact

If you have privacy questions about Balloons Popper, contact the publisher through the Google Play store listing contact details.
